Device Compatibility

Transfer EPUB to Your E-Reader

Quick guide to reading your converted EPUB on Kindle, Kobo, Apple Books, and Android.

How to Read Converted EPUB on Amazon Kindle

Amazon Kindle now natively supports EPUB files through Amazon's Send to Kindle system:

  1. Send to Kindle Web: Visit amazon.com/sendtokindle, drag your EPUB file, and click Send.
  2. Send to Kindle Email: Attach the converted EPUB file to an email and send it to your unique @kindle.com address found in your Amazon account settings.
  3. Sync your device: Connect your Kindle to Wi-Fi and tap Sync. The converted book will appear in your library with adjustable fonts and page flip animations!

How to Read Converted EPUB on Kobo E-Reader

  1. Connect your Kobo e-reader to your computer via USB cable.
  2. Tap Connect on your Kobo screen when prompted.
  3. Open your computer file explorer and drag the downloaded EPUB directly into the Kobo drive root or books folder.
  4. Eject your Kobo safely. The book will automatically be cataloged in your Kobo library.

How to Read Converted EPUB on Apple Books (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

  1. On iPhone or iPad: Tap the downloaded EPUB file in Safari and choose Open in Books.
  2. On Mac: Double-click the .epub file or drag it into the Apple Books application window.
  3. With iCloud Books enabled, the book will automatically sync across all your Apple devices.

How to Read Converted EPUB on Android

  1. Open Google Play Books, tap your profile icon, go to Play Books Settings, and check Enable PDF & EPUB uploading.
  2. Open the downloaded EPUB file with Google Play Books or upload via play.google.com/books/uploads.
  3. Alternatively, read offline with popular Android apps like Moon+ Reader, ReadEra, or Librera.
