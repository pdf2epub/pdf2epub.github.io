2. Conversion Fidelity & Reflow Limitations

PDF is a fixed-coordinate document standard designed for paper print reproduction, whereas EPUB is a reflowable digital publication format designed for dynamic screen scaling.

While PDF to EPUB Pro utilizes advanced heuristic algorithms to parse headings, sentences, paragraphs, and embedded imagery:

  • Scanned image-only PDFs without optical character recognition (OCR) text layers cannot be converted into reflowable text without prior OCR processing.
  • Heavily complex multi-column layouts, overlapping text boxes, mathematical formulas, and nested financial spreadsheets may require manual post-conversion review.
  • The service is provided without warranty regarding typographic precision or exact replica positioning.
