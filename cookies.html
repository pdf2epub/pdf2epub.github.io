Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026. How we utilize essential local storage to power your converter experience.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and local browser storage (such as HTML5 localStorage) are small text files or key-value entries stored on your computer, tablet, or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are commonly used to remember user preferences, maintain session state, or gather analytics data.

2. Our Strict Zero-Tracking Commitment

PDF to EPUB Pro does NOT use any third-party marketing cookies, cross-site trackers, fingerprinting scripts, or advertising networks.

Unlike competitor conversion portals that monetize your attention by serving invasive pop-ups and tracking your reading habits across the web, our tool operates on a clean, privacy-first model.

3. What We Store: Essential Functional Keys

We strictly use your browser's local storage (localStorage) for the following essential user interface preferences:

  • p2e_theme: Remembers whether you selected Dark Mode or Light Mode across visits so you do not experience sudden screen glare.
  • p2e_cookie_consent: Records that you have seen and responded to the cookie consent banner, preventing it from showing on every page visit.

ℹ️ Client-Side Only: None of this information is ever sent to our servers. It remains strictly sandboxed inside your local browser.

4. Cookie Consent Controls

When you first visit our site, our Cookie Banner allows you to:

  • Accept All: Enables local preference storage for your theme and settings.
  • Essential Only: Operates strictly with core functional requirements.

5. How to Clear or Disable Cookies in Your Browser

You have full control over your browser data. You can delete or block cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Clear browsing data > Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data > Clear Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data > Remove All.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Clear browsing data.

6. Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this Cookie Policy, you may contact our engineering team via the Contact Us page.
